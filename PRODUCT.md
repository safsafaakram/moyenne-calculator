# Product

## Register

product

## Users

First-year Algerian university students entering their own TD, TP, and exam scores to compute their semester average. They use this on mobile, often between classes or at home. The task is quick and focused: enter numbers, get the result.

## Product Purpose

A semester average calculator for Algerian faculty students. It computes module-level averages using the standard formula (Exam x 0.6 + CC x 0.4) and rolls them up into a weighted semester average. No accounts, no persistence, no extra features — type scores, see the result, done.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, reliable. The interface should feel like a trustworthy academic tool — precise without being cold, helpful without being noisy.

## Anti-references

No dark mode. No cluttered layouts. The calculator should not look like a generic Bootstrap template or a busy dashboard.

## Design Principles

- **Mobile-first, always.** Every layout decision starts at the smallest screen. Desktop is a bonus.
- **One thing at a time.** The primary action is data entry. Reduce visual noise so the student can focus on their scores.
- **Results speak loudest.** The semester average is the whole point. Make it impossible to miss.
- **Trust through clarity.** Numbers, labels, and formulas are transparent. No mystery math.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard legibility: sufficient contrast, readable font sizes. Reduced motion preferred.
